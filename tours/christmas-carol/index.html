A Christmas Carol: Dickens' London

Walk in Scrooge's footsteps through the gaslit City of London.

"Marley was dead, to begin with." So begins the most famous ghost story ever told, and so begins our walk. Starting in the shadow of St Paul's, we wind through the old City of London to the very streets Dickens had in mind for Ebenezer Scrooge, including the sites of his counting house and London residence.

Questions & answers

How much walking is involved?

About 1.5 miles (2.5 km) at a gentle pace over 90 minutes, with regular stops for stories. The route is mostly flat city streets, with some narrow alleys and courtyards.

Is the tour accessible?

Most of the route is step-free on pavements, but some old courts and alleys are narrow and have uneven paving or cobbles. Wheelchair and mobility scooter users are welcome; please contact us before booking so we can talk through the route with you. Benches are available at a few stops.

Where do we meet?

At the column in the middle of Paternoster Square, next to St Paul's Cathedral. Please arrive 10 minutes early. Your guide will be waiting and leaves promptly at the start time.

Where does it finish?

At Leadenhall Market, a short walk from Bank, Monument and Liverpool Street stations.

Does it go ahead in bad weather?

Yes, rain or shine. Please dress warmly, wear comfortable shoes and bring an umbrella.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes, children are very welcome. There are a few spooky ghost stories, so it suits ages 8 and up best.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Get in touch with us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to move you to another date.
